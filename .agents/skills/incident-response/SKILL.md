# Incident Response Skill

## Phases
Preparation, identification, analysis, containment planning, eradication planning, recovery and lessons learned.

## Workflow
1. Establish incident authority, systems and business impact.
2. Preserve volatile and nonvolatile evidence using approved procedures.
3. Build a timeline and identify affected assets/accounts.
4. Recommend containment with impact and rollback considerations.
5. Require human approval before changing systems or disabling accounts.
6. Record decisions and communications.

## Boundaries
No autonomous isolation, deletion, account lockout, remediation or system changes. Do not destroy evidence.

## Output
Incident summary, timeline, affected assets, indicators, confidence, containment options, recovery plan and evidence gaps.
