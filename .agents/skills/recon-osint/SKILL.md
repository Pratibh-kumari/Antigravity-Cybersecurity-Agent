# Reconnaissance and OSINT Skill

## Workflow
1. Confirm authorization and exact domain/IP scope.
2. Prefer passive sources and user-provided inventories.
3. Use DNS/WHOIS lookups only for approved domains and public information.
4. Record source, collection time, query, result and confidence.
5. Separate verified assets from inferred relationships.

## Boundaries
No scanning of unrelated infrastructure, account enumeration, phishing, social engineering, or collection of personal data. Do not bypass access controls.

## Deliverable
Asset inventory, exposed-service hypotheses, source citations, uncertainty and recommended defensive actions.
